# Hwarang: The Poet Warrior Youth

## About
**Hwarang** is a *historical K-drama* that aired on KBS2 in 2016. It is set in the Silla Kingdom and follows a group of young elite warriors called the Hwarang.

## Main Characters
1. Sun-woo (Park Seo-joon)
2. Ah-ro (Go Ara)
3. Sam-maek-jong (Park Hyung-sik)
4. Han-sung (V of BTS)

## Why I Like It
- Beautiful historical setting
- Strong friendships among the Hwarang
- Emotional story with romance and action

> A story of friendship, loyalty, and young warriors finding their purpose.
